Purpose & Scope of This Document

This document has been prepared for the senior leadership and human resources function of Fred's Ranch & Resort. It serves three purposes: first, to provide a plain-language account of the original draft Balanced Scorecard instrument as prepared for the Operations Manager role; second, to present the complete organisational structure of Fred's Ranch & Resort as ratified in July 2026; and third, to introduce and justify the new evaluation-ready Balanced Scorecard sheet that supersedes the draft.

Readers are assumed to hold operational or governance responsibility within a hotel or resort context. No prior acquaintance with Balanced Scorecard methodology is presupposed, but a working familiarity with hotel performance management is assumed.

This page is a technical and governance reference document. It does not constitute a contract of employment, nor does it modify any existing terms and conditions of service. Evaluation outputs generated using the enclosed instrument shall be governed by the property's applicable HR policy and any relevant collective bargaining agreements.

Legal Standing & Instrument Provenance

The Balanced Scorecard methodology was developed by Kaplan and Norton (1992) and is widely adopted as a performance management framework within the international hospitality sector. The instrument described herein is an original work product of Fred's Ranch & Resort's management team, adapted to the specific operational mandate of the Operations Manager as defined in the property's approved organisational structure dated July 2026.

Authorship and Ownership. This evaluation instrument, including all key performance indicators, weighting structures, measurement methodologies, and rating rubrics contained herein, constitutes proprietary management documentation belonging exclusively to Fred's Ranch & Resort. Reproduction, adaptation, or distribution to any third party without the written authorisation of the Board of Directors is prohibited.

Evidentiary Use. Completed evaluation sheets, once executed with signatures by all three parties named in the signature block (General Manager, People & Culture Manager, and the incumbent Operations Manager), shall constitute a formal performance record and may be relied upon in any subsequent employment proceeding, grievance process, or disciplinary matter in accordance with the property's HR policy framework.

Data Protection. Personal performance data recorded on any completed instance of this instrument is subject to the applicable data protection legislation of the jurisdiction in which Fred's Ranch & Resort operates. Access shall be limited to the named evaluators, the incumbent, and the Board of Directors. Retention shall be in accordance with the property's records management schedule.

The instrument covers the evaluation period designated at the time of use (mid-year or annual) and applies exclusively to the named incumbent. A separate, structurally identical instrument shall be prepared for each other post within the property's performance management programme.

The Original Draft Scorecard: What It Was

The source document — Freds_Ranch_Detailed_Operations_Manager_BSC_2026.xlsx — was prepared as a working draft to establish the strategic logic of the Operations Manager's performance framework. It articulated the role mandate, named the five performance dimensions, assigned dimension-level weights, and specified seventeen key performance indicators with their corresponding measurement methodologies and targets.

As a draft, the document served its primary purpose well: it established a rigorous, hospitality-appropriate framework grounded in operational discipline, SHIELD culture enforcement, systems adoption, and guest experience stewardship. However, it lacked the structural features necessary for deployment as an evaluation instrument — specifically, score input fields, a weighted scoring engine, a rating rubric, and a signature block constituting formal evidentiary record.

Strategic Role Statement (as defined in the original instrument). The Operations Manager is responsible for: daily operational consistency; SOP implementation and institutionalisation; service quality control; operational discipline; interdepartmental execution; guest experience consistency; safety and compliance; frontline SHIELD culture enforcement; and operational efficiency and continuous improvement. The Operations Manager is evaluated primarily as the custodian of operational excellence, discipline, service consistency, and frontline execution.

The Evaluation Sheet: Structure & Function

The evaluation-ready instrument (FRR_Operations_Manager_BSC_Evaluation_2026.xlsx) is the deployment successor to the original draft. It preserves all seventeen KPIs, all five dimension weights, and all targets verbatim from the source document. What it adds is the operational and evidentiary infrastructure required to make the framework a functioning performance management tool.

Original Draft — What It Had
  • Strategic role mandate statement
  • Five performance dimensions with dimension-level weights
  • 17 KPIs with methodology descriptions
  • Performance targets (qualitative and quantitative)
  • Readable tabular layout in Excel
Evaluation Sheet — What Is Added
  • Individual KPI weights (dimension weight ÷ KPI count)
  • Score input column (1–5 scale) with yellow input cells
  • Evidence / Notes column (free text per KPI)
  • Weighted Score formula: score × KPI_weight ÷ 5
  • Total weighted score (auto-summed, max = 1.00)
  • Rating band: auto-resolves to word verdict
  • Evaluator Remarks column
  • Meta block: evaluator, date, review type, overall score
  • Three-party signature block (legal execution)
  • Scoring guide rubric (1–5 definitions)
  • Brand-colour formatting, freeze panes, print-ready A3 landscape

The Scoring Engine

Each KPI is scored on a 1–5 integer scale. The weighted score for each KPI is calculated as:

Weighted Score = Score (1–5) × KPI Individual Weight ÷ 5

Where KPI Individual Weight = Dimension Weight ÷ Number of KPIs in that dimension.

Total Score = Σ all Weighted Scores  (maximum value = 1.00, representing a full score of 5/5 across all KPIs).

Rating Bands: 0.00–0.40 = Below Target  |  0.41–0.60 = Partially Meets  |  0.61–0.75 = Meets Target  |  0.76–0.85 = Exceeds Target  |  0.86–1.00 = Exceptional
All weighted score calculations are implemented as native Excel formulae and require no macros or external add-ins. The sheet recalculates on every score entry. No data is stored externally. The file is self-contained and portable.

Why This Version: The Design Rationale

The decision to build a purpose-designed evaluation sheet rather than annotate the original draft was guided by four considerations that are specific to the hospitality management context and the governance obligations of Fred's Ranch & Resort.

Evidentiary Integrity

A completed and signed evaluation must be capable of standing as a record in any employment proceeding. The original draft contained no signature provision and no mechanism for recording the evaluator's identity or the evaluation date. The new instrument satisfies these requirements in a form that is self-evidently a contemporaneous record.

Eliminates Manual Calculation

Weighted scoring performed manually introduces transcription error and creates audit risk. Formulae embedded in the sheet eliminate this: the evaluator records a score; the instrument computes the rest. This is not a convenience — it is a control.

Scalable Template Architecture

The layout is designed to be duplicated for every post in the ExCo and HOD layer. The column structure, colour coding, formula logic, and signature block are identical across all derivatives. Only the title bar, role context band, KPIs, and weights change per post. This ensures institutional consistency across the entire performance management cycle.

Organisational Alignment

The instrument situates the Operations Manager precisely within the confirmed organisational structure: reporting to Hiram Nyingi (General Manager), with the evaluation countersigned by Reagan Ogol (People & Culture) and acknowledged by the incumbent (Albert Maroa). This triangulation reflects the governance hierarchy as ratified in the July 2026 organogram.

Mid-Year & Annual Flexibility

The review type field accommodates both mid-year and annual evaluation cycles without modification to the instrument. This is consistent with best practice in East African hospitality management, where a mid-year check-in against targets reduces end-of-year surprises and supports progressive performance management.

Faithful to the Source Framework

No KPI has been added, removed, or reworded from the original draft. The dimension weights are preserved exactly. The instrument does not reinterpret the management's intent — it operationalises it. The source document remains the authoritative statement of what is being measured; the evaluation sheet is the mechanism for measuring it.
