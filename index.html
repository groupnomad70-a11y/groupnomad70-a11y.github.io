Purpose & Scope of This Document

This document has been prepared for the senior leadership and human resources function of Fred's Ranch & Resort. It serves three purposes: to explain the original scorecard logic, to present the operational organogram that governs it, and to provide a deployment-ready evaluation sheet for the Operations Manager role.

It is intended as a practical governance and management reference. The content is written in plain language so that it can be used by management, supervisors, and evaluators without prior specialist knowledge of scorecard methodology.

This page is a technical and governance reference document. It does not constitute a contract of employment, nor does it amend any terms and conditions of service. Evaluation outputs generated using the enclosed instrument shall be governed by the property's applicable HR policy and relevant collective bargaining agreements.

Legal Standing & Instrument Provenance

The Balanced Scorecard method was developed by Kaplan and Norton and is commonly used in the international hospitality sector. The instrument described herein reflects an original managerial framework designed for Fred's Ranch & Resort and adapted to the operational mandate of the Operations Manager.

Authorship and Ownership. This evaluation instrument, including all KPI definitions, weights, measurement methods, and rating rubrics, constitutes proprietary management documentation belonging exclusively to Fred's Ranch & Resort.

Evidentiary Use. Completed evaluation sheets, once executed with signatures by the General Manager, People & Culture Manager, and the incumbent, shall constitute a formal performance record and may be relied upon in a grievance or disciplinary process.

Data Protection. Personal performance data recorded on any completed instance of this instrument is subject to the applicable data protection legislation of the jurisdiction in which the property operates.

The Original Draft Scorecard: What It Was

The source document, Freds_Ranch_Detailed_Operations_Manager_BSC_2026.xlsx, established the strategic logic of the Operations Manager performance framework. It listed the role mandate, the five performance dimensions, the weighted KPI structure, and the measurement logic against which performance would be assessed.

As a draft, it served its purpose well: it created a disciplined framework grounded in operations, culture, systems adoption, guest experience, and continuous improvement. It needed a formal deployment structure to become an implementable evaluation tool.

Strategic Role Statement. The Operations Manager is responsible for daily operational consistency, SOP implementation, service quality control, operational discipline, front-line SHIELD culture enforcement, systems adoption, and operational efficiency.

The Evaluation Sheet: Structure & Function

The evaluation-ready instrument preserves all 17 KPIs from the original draft while adding the operational and evidentiary infrastructure required to make the framework usable as a performance management tool.

  • Strategic role statement
  • Five dimensions with weights
  • 17 KPIs and targets
  • Measurement logic
Evaluation Sheet
  • Individual KPI score input (1–5)
  • Evidence and notes column

All weighted score calculations are implemented as native Excel formulas and require no macros or external add-ins. The sheet recalculates on every score entry. No data is stored externally.

Why This Version: The Design Rationale

The decision to build a purpose-designed evaluation sheet rather than annotate the original draft was guided by four practical requirements specific to hospitality management and governance.

Evidentiary Integrity

A complete and signed evaluation must be capable of standing as a record in any employment proceeding. The new instrument includes the signature block and date fields required to make it self-evidently evidential.

Eliminates Manual Calculation

Weighted scoring, total score, and banding are handled automatically so the evaluator records a score and the instrument calculates the rest. This reduces transcription error and risk.

Scalable Template Architecture

The layout is structured so it can be duplicated for every post across the executive and HOD layer without changing the controlling logic. Only the title, role context, KPIs, and weights change by role.

Organisational Alignment

The evaluation sits within the confirmed reporting hierarchy: the Operations Manager reports to the General Manager, with the review countersigned by People & Culture and acknowledged by the incumbent. This reflects the governance hierarchy captured in the organogram.
